## Subscription and domain entitlement

The approved offer is EUR 19 per month or EUR 190 per year, excluding VAT. An ordinary supported .nl registration or incoming transfer and continuing ordinary renewals are included while the subscription remains eligible. Other supported extensions may carry a surcharge, explicitly quoted and accepted before the chargeable operation. No mailbox or assisted website migration is sold as part of this offer.

You may subscribe before choosing a domain at the same subscription price. An unused included-domain entitlement remains available while eligible. It is not cash, a refundable credit or a second subscription. Selecting a domain does not itself authorize a public website. Registration, transfer, HTTPS, customer approval and publication each retain their own prerequisites. Existing domains and mail DNS are not discarded during website cleanup.

## Private workspace and publication

Verified free access provides the private AI builder, preview and checkout. A financially secured paid subscription provides role-appropriate manual editing before public activation, including without a domain. Save stores private work; Publish is a separate authorized action. Collaborators share the tenant's rights and allowance; invitations and account recovery do not grant another subscription or allowance. Public activation still requires its own readiness and approval.

## AI allowance and uncertainty

Free accounts and paid tenants each have an absolute monthly allowance capped at USD 1 of metered provider cost. The paid allowance is shared by the tenant's owner and editors. Billed failed attempts consume allowance. Unresolved possible charges remain reserved until reconciled; availability is not increased by an unknown outcome.

The product shows the usable percentage, reset time and pending or recovery state rather than a monetary balance. This allowance is not stored value or a cash entitlement. Free cycles follow the verified-account anniversary; paid cycles follow the first confirmed payment, including annual subscriptions and calendar short months. The first confirmed paid transition ends free availability and starts one full paid allowance once. Duplicate callbacks, new devices, domain selection, invitations or agreement recovery cannot repeat that allocation. Unused allowance does not roll over. At exhaustion, free preview/checkout and eligible paid manual tools remain available; new AI work waits for reset or reconciliation.

## Coverage, cancellation and arrears

Cancellation ends at the committed paid coverage boundary without an extra notice-month charge. Recoverable site data remains available for a 30-day recovery/export window, subject to separately retained legal, financial, security and domain evidence.

During continuous overdue days 0–13, existing public/manual service remains, while new AI and chargeable purchases pause. On day 14 public service and ordinary editing suspend; billing recovery, exports, invoices and domain-custody rights remain reachable. On day 30 the subscription terminates, followed by 30 further recovery days. Ordinary purge is no earlier than day 60. Cleanup rechecks eligibility and outstanding obligations; it is resumable and does not silently destroy a domain or existing mail routing.

## Inactive free work and retained features

Unpaid work becomes eligible for cleanup after 30 days of meaningful inactivity plus a delivered seven-day warning. Failed delivery is not notice. Passive polling or background jobs do not count as meaningful customer activity.

Forms have a 90-day default retention policy. Appointment records have a 90-day default after appointment end, configurable from 30 to 730 days. Optional Google/Microsoft calendar synchronization requires the owner's authorization; disconnect, retry and recovery follow the current supported integration contract. Analytics, cookie choices and tenant privacy disclosures remain separately governed. Customers supply accurate controller details and decide whether to enable their tenant disclosure; custom text is not silently overwritten.
