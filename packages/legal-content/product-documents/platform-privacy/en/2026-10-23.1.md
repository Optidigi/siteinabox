## Retention and recovery

The approved product lifecycle retains meaningfully active free work; unpaid cleanup requires 30 inactive days and a delivered seven-day warning. Normal cancellation ends at committed coverage and provides 30 days for recovery/export. Continuous arrears preserve existing public/manual service on days 0–13, suspend ordinary public/editing access on day 14, terminate on day 30 and retain recovery for 30 further days. Domain custody and existing mail DNS are preserved. Financial, legal and security evidence can have separate justified schedules.

Forms have a 90-day default. Appointments have a 90-day default after their end, with a tenant choice of 30–730 days. Legal/financial records and optional-calendar correlation, token and outbox data need their own accurate schedule. Backup governance targets an encrypted off-host rolling 30-day window; actual implementation and restore/deletion evidence must be verified before publication.

The platform's analytics governance targets 13 months with enforcement. Current provider query-window and deletion assurance must be verified: a query-history limit is not deletion evidence, and no historical/default retention value may substitute for missing current readback.
